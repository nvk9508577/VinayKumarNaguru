02 / Engagement

How service delivery works

Not a promise of end-to-end ownership for every platform. Scope is agreed first, and work is performed within approved access, change controls and the customer's operating model.

01 · Understand

Clarify the symptom, affected users, business impact, recent changes and success criteria.

02 · Diagnose

Use logs, configuration, health checks and a layered troubleshooting path to narrow the cause.

03 · Remediate

Apply an approved, proportionate fix; escalate specialist tasks when they fall outside the agreed scope.

04 · Validate & hand over

Confirm service restoration, document what changed and share prevention or follow-up actions.

The listed services describe areas of capability and potential support. Specific deliverables, technologies, service levels and ownership should be agreed for each customer engagement.
