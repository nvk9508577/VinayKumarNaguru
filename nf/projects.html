03 / Operating approach

How I work through problems

The value of a lab is not just the number of tools installed. It is the practice of understanding dependencies, making controlled changes, and verifying the result.

01 · Establish the facts

Check the symptom, scope, logs, service status, network path and available resources before changing configuration.

02 · Isolate and test

Separate host, storage, application, DNS, routing and remote-access possibilities; change one thing at a time where practical.

03 · Validate and document

Confirm the service works from the intended access path, record what changed, and note remaining limitations or follow-up work.

Continuous improvement: this lab is not “finished.” I keep extending it as time, hardware and learning priorities allow. Project status is intentionally labelled so visitors can distinguish maintained services from experiments.
